# Credits

## Art, sound and music
Everything in Dead Acre so far is original and made in code: all shapes, textures
(dirt, planks, stone, plaster, shingles, straw, barrel paint, clothing), the
characters, the sky and the lighting. Every sound (gunshots, impacts, footsteps,
zombie voices) is synthesised live by the game with the Web Audio API.
No outside images, models, sounds or music are used yet.

When real models, sounds and music are added (Phase 7), each one will be listed here
with its creator, where it came from and its license.
